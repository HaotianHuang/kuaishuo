# P3: the first session and placement (contracts), with P2's reviews put right

README.md §6 and algorithm.md §3 give the reasons; p2.md is the ranker as built. Where they disagree, this file wins
for P3. Three builders work at once, each on its own files (below); nobody edits another's, and nobody edits the

## What a new phone sees

1. **It opens on the feed.** No hash is For You (the Mix, dealt by rank.js). The five versions (the launcher) move to
   `#lab`. `#mix` stays the Mix with its back button (to `#lab`), for the lab and the tests.
2. **The sound gate is the hook.** A phone that has never played sees its first card's premise on the gate: for
   airport-esim, "You've just landed at Pudong, and your phone has no signal." over "Tap to start · sound on". A
   phone that has played sees the gate as today.
3. **Card 1 is the hook story,** airport-esim (a scenario with `first: true` wins, if a build has one; neither: R1's
   pick). Its first freeze comes about 7 s after the tap. **Its first line has more help than the card's rung:** the
   phone's first line ever plays at A4 (its frame free, no clock), or A5 (said for you first) if the simulator says
   new learners win it under 85% of the time at A4 (measure; the rule picks). The rest of card 1 plays at the card's
   rung.
4. **Card 2 asks about you,** once a phone, on one card, every question a tap and every one skippable (a swipe keeps
   what was tapped):
   - "Growing up, did you hear Chinese at home?": Mandarin / Cantonese or another dialect / I studied some / I'm new
     to it (learner.persona(): 'mandarin' | 'dialect' | 'studied' | 'new').
   - "What would you love to do in Chinese?" (any of): Talk with family / Travel / Read and write / Just for fun
     (`ks.goals`: a list of 'family' | 'travel' | 'read' | 'fun'; `[]` none chosen; absent: not asked).
   - "What year were you born?": a year, or "Rather not say" (`ks.born`: a year, 0 for rather not, absent: not
     asked). P5 reads it (under 13: no ads, ever). Nothing leaves the phone.
   Questions already answered (Settings → Your Chinese has the first since P1) aren't asked again; with all three
   answered there's no card. Settings → Your Chinese shows and changes all three.
5. **The next stories place you** (README's "cards 2–5"): see Placement, below.
6. **Session 1 has no ads** (none exist yet) **and no permission prompt the player didn't start** (the microphone's
   comes when they tap the mic, as now).

## Placement and the hook: app/shared/rank.js (builder B)

- **The hook:** a phone that has never played (no model lines, no srs entry, no story done) gets the hook story as card
  1, `why.rule` 'hook', at its base level and the rung `startAt()` gives (A2 on a blank model).
- **The about card:** `{ kind: 'about', ask }` as card 2 of the visit in which the hook was dealt (or the first card of
  a later visit, if that visit ended after card 1), while any question is unanswered and the card has never been
  dealt on this phone (`ks.arms.about`: when it was dealt). `why.rule` 'about'. Like a recap: no format (R2 skips it),
  none of the 40% (R4), not one of R5's first 3 cards' extras, not a review (R3).
- **Placing:** from the hook until 12 placement lines (first attempts on story cards, not beside the story) have been
  answered, across visits if the first was short, or g's sd is under 0.4. While placing:
  - a story's τ is 0.70 (its item.tau, so assisted() aims its rung there: the most informative point while mostly
    winning);
  - its V is the information its lines carry about the skills the model is least sure of: Σ over its lines of
    p(1 − p)·(a·C·a), a the way's loadings (learner.js), rescaled 0–1 over the candidates;
  - **PL1:** one of the placement stories is a call, where R7 allows one (a heritage answer, or lines that say so);
  - **PL2:** one is a chat (reading);
  - **PL3, the bonus:** once, after a card whose first attempts were all right (y ≥ 0.9), never after a miss or a loss
    and not in the first 3 cards: `{ kind: 'bonus', p }`, a phrase from a story not played yet, at level
    clamp(⌈say⌉ + 1, 3, 5) (the build's nearest level that has one), the one whose predicted chance at A2 is nearest
    0.5. `why.rule` 'bonus'. Its format is review (R2); it's none of the 40% and no review for R3; missed, it isn't a
    loss (τ's 0.92 and the end on a high don't count it). Its line row carries `task: 'bonus'` (cards.js): a first
    meeting, so the learner model counts it whole, not at a review's half.
- **Goals:** `store.get('goals')` (kv). A goal's topics start their arms at 0.8 (Beta(3.2, 0.8)) instead of 0.7:
  family → family, home, celebrations, dating; travel → travel, food, shopping. read → the chat format's prior and
  write's +0.08; fun → scene's and chase's +0.05. V's gaps weigh family's L, S, V ×1.3 and read's R, W ×1.5
  (algorithm.md §3). data/ has no topics: there goals act through formats and gaps.
- **v1's warmup** (deal-v1.js): after 5 cards with no review in a visit, the longest phrase met this visit (not due
  yet) comes back early as a flash crisis, once a visit, where the rules allow a review. `why.rule` 'warmup'.
- **Content request 5, the cards half:** picks.js `wordsToWrite(data, scn)` and `linesToShadow(scn)` take the story as
  played (tailored: its lines are `scn/beat@L` phrases where a level overrides them), and the ranker passes it so; a
  flash crisis of an `@L` phrase is dealt as that phrase (cards.js restages it: builder A).
- R1–R7 stand. The hook and the about card are R1's first card and an exempt card: rules.mjs learns both.

## P2's reviews put right (builder B, first)

As built, For You serves 7–17% of due reviews within two visits (v1 12–21%): fewer than v1 on p4b-2 for every
persona. Measure these levers, alone and together, on data/, p4b-2 and p4b-3, seeds 1–3, with the audit:
- **runs of up to ten** phrases (five now);
- **reviews at a rung:** a flash crisis or a run predicted, and played, at the rung the ladder would give its lines
  (assist.startAt for the phrase), so the 0.55 floor lets a weak learner's reviews through; srs credit stays capped by
  free help as it is (a free frame 2★);
- **one rematch a visit** ahead of new stories.
Ship the combination serving the most due reviews such that, on every build and seed: D7 ≥ v1 × 1.25, skill gain ≥
v1's, ECE ≤ 0.05, line success 0.72–0.88, 0 rule breaks; and For You serves at least v1's share for every persona.
None meets them all: ship the one that serves most with D7 ≥ v1 × 1.25, and say which bars it misses. Playing a
review at a rung needs cards.js and chase.js to read the rung: builder A wires it (`ctx.assist` on a review, run and
bonus card: `assist.forLines(ids, { mode, tau })` → `{ A, knobs }`, the rung `startAt()` gives those lines now, moving
nothing: no step of the ladder's budget spent, `last()` unchanged; its rows carry the rung as `assist`, as a story's
do). mix.js plays a review at it only where the item says so (`item.A`), so either answer of the measurement ships
without more wiring. Everything measured goes into p3.md's as-built section.

### The first line: FIRST_LINE

The simulator plays the hook's first freeze, first try, for every learner and records its true chance at A4 and at A5.
These are base-assumption ranges over the nine reports:

| persona | true chance at A4 | at A5 | right as played (A5) |
|---|---|---|---|
| mandarin | 88–95% | 96–98% | 96–100% |
| dialect | 75–90% | 91–97% | 93–97% |
| studied | 70–87% | 88–96% | 85–97% |
| new | 43–69% | 72–88% | 74–89% |
| advanced | 96–99% | 99–100% | 99–100% |

The rule is A4 if new learners win the line 85% of the time there, else A5. At A4 they win 43–69%, so **FIRST_LINE =
5** (assist.js, builder A's constant, set from these numbers).

Even at A5, new learners fall short of 85% on 6 of the 9 runs. The spread between seeds (72–74% on seed 1, 85–88% on
seeds 2 and 3) is that one line's true difficulty, which each seed's world draws afresh. A5 is the top rung, so its
true chance is the bound: no rung does better.

### Restated, with the bound

- **Placement at its end, ≥ 90% within one level.** The best estimator knows each persona's true mean and every
  line's true difficulty. After placement's lines it reaches 96–97% for say, but only 74–75% for listen and 74% for
  read. So 90% for listen and read is beyond any estimator from these lines.
  - Restated: within one level for at least 90% of the share the bound allows, on the bound's own learners.
  - As built, on those learners: say 71–84% (0.74–0.88 of the bound), listen 47–61% (0.64–0.81), read 21–45%
    (0.28–0.59). All three miss even the restated bar, read by most.
  - By the about card's answer (the review round's numbers, below), learners who told it right are placed within one
    level 88–100% of the time for say; a wrong answer or none is what pulls say and listen down.
  - Overall (all learners) read is 73–80%, because learners at the bottom are easy to place.
