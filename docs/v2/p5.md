# P5: retention loops, then the economy: hearts, revives and rewarded ads (contracts)

README.md §7 and §8 and retention.md §1, §2 and §6 give the reasons and the numbers; p2.md and p3.md are the ranker and
the first session as built. Where they disagree, this file wins for P5. P5 runs in three parts, each built, reviewed,
fixed, committed and published on its own, in this order (the owner's, 31 Oct 2026): **P5a, the loops** (days,
streaks, the daily challenge, progress you can see, series that drop a day at a time, and four of the owner's picks:

hosting are P6's, and each needs the owner's OK.

## P5a: the loops

### What P3 left for P5a

- **Phones that played before P3 are asked about themselves once** (p3.md F13). rank.js deals the about card
  (`{ kind: 'about' }`, as P3 built it) once to a phone that has played but never seen it and has a question unasked:
  never as a visit's first card, never straight after a loss, at most once a visit, and marked seen
  (`ks.arms.about`) when it comes on screen, as P3 does. P5b's ads need the year (none under 13); with the card seen
  and the year still unasked, P5b's first offer asks it in place, as below.
- **The menu** (main.js, P3) gains Situations (`#situations`) and Today's challenge (`#challenge`), with the counts.
- **Answers about you** are announced with P3's `'ks:you'` event; a year taken back is removed (`kv.del`), so absent
  means not asked everywhere.

### days.js (new, DOM-free)

```js
import * as days from './days.js'
days.init({ store = kv, now = time.now })   // listens to events.js rows (events.on); Node runs it with over()'s stores
days.today()            // events.js dayKey(): the day starts at 4 am, so a late night counts for that evening
days.streak()           // { n, best, freezes, kept (today), risk (yesterday unkept, nothing spent yet), broke: { day, n } | null }
days.played()           // days this phone has played on; days.stories(): stories finished; days.since(): days since install
days.released(scn)      // a series episode is out: episode 1 always; episode k the day after k − 1 was finished
days.challenge(day?)    // { n, id, mod, day }: today's 今日挑战 (below)
```

- **The streak:** a day is kept by one story finished (a card row, kind scn, state done) or three review lines answered
  (flash crises, a run's lines, a word written again). `ks.streak = { install, kept: [day…] (the last 60), freezes,
  spent: [day…], best }` (not `ks.days`: events.js's daily totals live there).
- **Freezes:** one earned at each 7-day milestone (7, 14, 21 …), two held at most, spent by themselves on a missed day
  between two played ones (the first visit after it says so, once: "Your streak held: a freeze covered Tuesday. 1
  left."). Repair (a broken streak mended within 48 h) is P5b's offer; P5a only records `broke`.
- **Episodes a day at a time:** `released()` is passed into the ranker as an option (`released(scn)`, like `mode`), so
  rank.js's `open(s)` deals an episode only once it's out; the recap's and end card's teaser say "Episode 4 · tomorrow"
  while it isn't. The ranker never imports days.js.

### 今日挑战, the daily challenge

- `days.challenge()`: the number counts days from 2026-11-01 (#1); the story is `hash(day) % pool` over the stories
  every learner can be dealt (base level ≤ 2, no series episodes), the same on every phone; the modifier alternates
  `panic` (the clock at A0's ×0.7, whatever the rung) and `quiet` (no pinyin, no English). Played at the learner's own
  say level (content.tailor to the nearest level the story has to `learner.level().say`), so everyone plays the same
  moment.
- **Dealt** once a day, as a story card (`{ kind: 'challenge', scn, lv, mod, n }`, `why.rule` 'challenge'): not as a
  visit's first card, R2 by its format, a story for R4 and R5, never the hook or in placement, and only if not played
  today. The menu also opens it (`#challenge`).
- **Its result:** a line a square: 🟩 right first time with no help beyond the rung's, 🟨 right with help or on a second
  try, 🟥 missed; the share text is `快说 #42 🐯 🟩🟩🟨🟩🟥 4/5` (the story's stage emoji), copied with a toast, or
  `navigator.share({ text })` where the phone has it. No link, no ID: nothing about the player in it.
- It's free: it costs no heart (P5b), win or lose.

### Progress you can see

- **Situations you can handle:** a story won at ≥ 2★ whose phrases have all reached srs box ≥ 3 (recalled after a day).
  `#situations` lists them as stamps, newest first; the count shows in the recap and the menu.
- **The phrase deck:** `#phrases` (families grouped since P3) shows each phrase's box as a ring (0–6), and its head
  "37 phrases · 14 solid" (box ≥ 4).
- **Then and now:** srs entries keep `hist`, the last 8 `[day, stars]` (store.js `srs.record`, backward compatible). A
  phrase missed (≤ 1★) on an earlier day and said at 3★ now gets a stamp on its verdict, "Tuesday: missed. Today:
  nailed.", once a phrase, and a row in the recap.
- **The recap** gains: the streak and freezes; the share of lines from memory this week against last; "3 situations to
  lock in tomorrow" (won stories with phrases due by tomorrow's end); the next episode's day.

### The owner's picks (31 Oct 2026)

- **Quiet mode, "Can't talk now":** speaking is the point, but a phone on a train can't talk aloud, and a skipped
  speaking card is a lost visit. A 🤫 button on every speaking answer panel (and Settings) switches the visit to
  answers with no voice: the learner's typed mode if they've typed before (pinyin, or characters where their read
  level is ≥ 2), else tiles; calls and chats still play (listening with subtitles is fine on a train: earphones); no
  shadow card is dealt. It lasts until the visit ends (rank.js's GAP) or it's tapped off, and the ranker is told it as
  it's told `mode` (`quiet()`). The learner model hears the modes as answered, as it does today.
- **Endings to collect:** a story's endings are its terminal steps a run can reach, wins and named losses alike. The end
  card says "Ending 1 of 3 found" (only where a story has more than one) with a nudge that saying something else
  changes what happens; the deck and `#situations` show the count. `days.endings(scn)` reads them from the log alone.
  The ranker, as it stands, counts a story with endings unfound as a rematch worth playing (a small V, as a due review
  is), never more than once a visit. Secret endings (a perfect line opening a hidden branch) are content's, P5c.
- **A countdown to a real day:** Settings → Your Chinese and the later about card ask "Anything coming up?": Lunar New
  Year with family (6 Feb 2027, 26 Jan 2028, 13 Feb 2029, 3 Feb 2030), a trip, a wedding or birthday, a call or visit,
  with a date (or none). `ks.soon = { what, day }`. The menu and the recap show "Lunar New Year in 23 days", and the
  recap "14 family lines solid"; as the day nears (30 days out and closer), the goal it implies (family, or travel)
  weighs its topics' arms and gaps more, as P3's goals do. Past the day it asks how it went once (😂 / 😳 / ❤️, kept
  as a count only) and offers the next.
- **Your Chinese's age:** the joke heritage speakers make about themselves, made a measure: `learner.level()` as an
  age, say and listen apart ("You understand like a 10-year-old and talk like a 5-year-old"): level 0 is 2, 1 is 4, 2
  is 6, 3 is 9, 4 is 12, 5 is 16, in between by the continuous level, never shown before placement ends. In Your
  Chinese, in the recap when one rises a year, and as share text (`我的中文：听力10岁，口语5岁 · 快说`, nothing about
  the player in it). Warm, never a grade: the copy never says "only" or "still".

### P5a as built: the logic (31 Oct 2026)

The contract above stands; this is what the logic builder made of it, and the choices it left. Each module's header
has the exact shapes.

- **app/shared/days.js** (new, DOM-free): `init({ data, store, now })` on the phone (after `learner.init`; it hears
  every row events.js logs, and the first time replays the log and backfills episode drops from the marks), and
  `createDays({ data, store, now, rows })` for Node. `today, streak, told, played, stories, since, released, challenge,
  result, endings, endKey, soon, setSoon, went, lunar, hear`. Kept in `ks.streak`, `ks.ends`, `ks.drops`, `ks.soon`,
  `ks.went`. Ad rows are never heard.
- **The streak:** a day is kept by one story card done (kind scn or challenge) or three review lines (a first attempt,
  task 'review', not skipped; a word written again). Freezes are earned at 7, 14, 21 … days, two held at most. They're
  spent as the phone first plays after a gap, one a missed day, and only when the gap is no longer than the freezes
  held. A longer gap breaks the streak (`broke: { day, n }`) and spends none. A day a freeze covered bridges the run
  but adds nothing to n. `risk` and a gap that can no longer be covered are worked out as the streak stands now, so
  the menu is right before the phone plays. `held` is the freeze notice until `told()`, and only of the day the phone
  came back: a later day played or the streak lost clears it, and the first time's replay of an older log tells
  nothing (the review found a phone from before P5a told of a freeze in September, its streak long broken).
- **The challenge:** the pool is the stories with base level ≤ 2 and no series, by id. The pick is FNV-1a with
  murmur's finaliser over the day key, % the pool, and the next one in the pool where that's the day before's story. #1
  is 1 Nov 2026 (today, 31 Oct, is #0), odd panic, even quiet. `played` means a run of it was finished that day; the
  result and its share text are the finished run's. A run left part-way (one line, then Back, a swipe or the page
  hidden) is neither: `#challenge` plays it on, each line answered keeping its first square, and a line the card was
  left on (cancelled) isn't squared. Once finished, playing it again changes nothing. `pending(id)` is the card on
  screen's squares as its row will record them, for the end screen. The squares come from its lines' rows: 🟩 is a
  first attempt right with no hint paid, no second try and the answer not heard. Pools: data/ 12, p4b-2 20, p4b-3 12.
- **Endings:** a story's end steps over every branch walked, keyed by `endKey(st)` = st.zh, else st.en (unique in
  every build; tailored levels share them). A card row names its ending as `end`. A row without one counts as the
  story's only ending of the row's outcome, where it has only one; otherwise nothing is counted.
- **Episodes:** `released(scn)`: episode 1 always; episode k the day after episode k − 1 was first finished.
- **The countdown:** `soon()` → `{ what, day, left, goal, near, past }`. rank.js reads ks.soon itself and weighs the
  implied goal by w = (31 − days left) / 31 inside 30 days: the goal's topics' prior +0.1·w, its gaps' weights ×(1 +
  (gap − 1)·w), over P3's goals.
- **rank.js:** it takes four new options, `released, quiet, challenge, endings`, and never imports days.js.
  - The challenge is dealt at the first place the rules allow after the end on a high: never as a visit's first card,
    while placing, or straight after a loss. R2 applies by its format, and R3, R6 and R7 apply. Rule 'challenge'.
  - It's aimed at a loss's τ (0.92, `CHALLENGE_TAU`, which the menu's `#challenge` plays at too), and dealt only where
    it's in reach as played: R6's floor, p̄ ≥ 0.55, holds at the rung under the one the ladder gives. The modifier
    takes about a rung's help away, and the story is the day's, not one picked for its fit. Without this, new
    learners' line success fell 0.02 (0.69 to 0.67 at its worst on data/). The menu opens it for anyone, in reach or
    not: it's the day's, and it's free.
  - The item is `{ kind: 'challenge', scn (tailored), lv, A, tau, mod, n, day }`. lv is the level nearest the say
    level, the lower of two as near. It has no cls, so it is no visit's rematch, and moves no arm. Once on screen, the
    day goes in ks.arms.challenge.
  - Until then its story is saved for it, so R1 or the score can't take the visit's one go at that story and leave the
    day without its challenge.
  - Episodes are candidates only once released. The recap's `next` gains `out`.
  - Quiet mode deals no shadow card.
  - A played story with endings unfound (of > 1) is cls 'ending', V 0.5, once a visit, and never ahead of a story new
    to you (rule 'later'). No rule gives way for one (format*, surest*), so a learner far below every story, with
    every story played, still gets the end card. rank-wire-check showed this.
  - The later about card asks home, goals, born and soon, those unanswered, with `why.later`. It goes once to a phone
    with no hook and no about seen: never first, never straight after a loss, after the challenge.
  - `session()` gains `challenge` and `quiet`.
- **learner.js:** `age(place)` → `{ say, listen }` in whole years (floored, by the table above), null before placement
  ends. A phone with no placement needs 12 lines in the model first. The old internal `age` is now `drift`. At the
  start, as listen/say: mandarin 7/4, dialect 3/2, studied 2/2, new 2/2.
- **store.js:** each srs entry keeps `hist`, its last 8 `[day, stars]`, with the day from 4 am. That's one entry a day,
  holding its lowest stars, so a day missed once stays missed. Every pick parses the whole srs store, and eight entries
  for every answer had cost about 0.1 ms of a pick's 0.4.
- **The checks:**
  - tools/days-check.mjs (new): the day, the streak's every case, 730 days of challenges on three builds, squares and
    share text, episodes, endings, soon, hist, age, and the phone's path under KS_NOW.
  - rank-check's new `loops` part, and the referee (tools/rules.mjs), which works out the challenge owed, drops,
    endings, quiet mode and the later about card from the rows.
  - tools/sim.mjs: P5a for For You only. A tenth of visits are quiet, each line answered with no voice: typed by a
    learner without a microphone (one who types), tiles by one with one (who never has). The challenge is played a
    rung harder. `--loops=off` deals For You as P3 left it (the referee then holds no P5a rule), `--quiet=` sets the
    quiet share, and the report has a loops table.
- **The sims (seed 1, n = 200, audited):** 0 breaks on all three builds.
  - data/ against its seed-1 copy: every passing row passes, and every failing row is within 0.01 or a point. Line
    success is 0.69 at its worst, as it was. ECE is 0.059 (0.058), and first meetings 0.116 (0.108). The first line
    is 74% (75%). Placement and due reviews are as good or better.
  - p4b-3 (whose numbers loops-off reproduces exactly): within the same bounds.
  - p4b-2's content changed after its copy was made, so loops-off on today's build is the reference. Against it,
    three failing rows dip 2 points: placement after 12 lines (say 51%, from 53%; read 52%, from 54%), placed within
    one level (listen 57%, from 59%), and due reviews served (31%, from 33%). `--quiet=0` puts placement back
    exactly. On seeds 2 and 3 the same cells move −2 to +3 with a mean of about 0, so this reads as a tenth of

  - The challenge comes 0.5–0.8 times a visit (one visit a day; none while placing). For new learners it's 0.3–0.4,
    since the day's story is often out of reach as played. It's won 83–97% of the time. Ending rematches come
    0–0.03 a visit, and quiet visits get no shadow cards.

## P5b: the economy: hearts, revives and rewarded ads

The owner's call (31 Oct 2026): free to play, like Candy Crush. Nobody is ever asked to pay; the game earns from
rewarded video ads a player chooses to watch, most of all to **revive** a story they've just lost and carry on from
the line they lost it on. There is no purchase and no subscription. README.md §8's rules stand where this section
doesn't change them: opt-in buttons that name their reward, never during a line, no interstitials, banners or
pre-rolls, and the ranker behind a wall.

### Hearts

- **Five hearts** (❤️ in the feed's top bar). A story lost and not revived costs one. One comes back every 30 minutes
  (time.js's clock, so KS_NOW moves it), and practice wins one back: every 3 review lines answered right (flash crises,
  a run's lines, a word written again), so learning what you've met refills what losing costs.
- **Out of hearts,** new stories and rematches wait; reviews, writes, shadows and the daily challenge never do. The next
  place a new story would take is the **hearts card** (`{ kind: 'hearts' }`, dealt by mix.js where the ranker returns a
  story the economy won't allow, the ranker asked again with `fresh()` false for the card after it): when the next heart
  comes, practice to win one now, or watch an ad for one.
- **No heart is lost** during the honeymoon, or on a phone with ads off (under 13, or no year given), so neither ever
  meets the hearts card.

### econ.js (new, DOM-free), ads.js and ads-mock.js (new)

```js
econ.init({ store = kv, now, days })        // ks.econ = { day, hearts, at, views, revives, last, mute: { placement: until }, nofill }
econ.state()            // { honeymoon, adsOn, hearts, next (ms to the next heart), revives, views }
econ.offer(placement)   // { show, free, why }: whether an offer button may show here now, and whether it's free
econ.fresh()            // a new story may be dealt now (a heart left): the ranker's `fresh()` option, all it knows
econ.lost(scn) / econ.practised(n)          // a story lost, not revived (a heart); review lines answered right
econ.grant(placement)   // the reward, once the ad has been watched (or no ad came: below)
ads.watch(placement)    // → Promise<{ rewarded, why }>: asks econ, stops the sound, locks the feed, shows the provider's ad, grants
// a provider: { init(), ready(placement) → bool, show(placement) → Promise<{ rewarded, why }> }; ads-mock.js is v2's only one
```

- **The honeymoon:** until 3 days played and 15 stories finished (days.js), no offers at all and no heart lost; a
  revive is free, 3 a day.
- **Ads on** only with a birth year at least 13 years back (`ks.born`, P3). With none given, the first offer that
  would show asks for it in place (the about card's picker), once; "Rather not say", or under 13: ads off for good,
  with the honeymoon's rules kept.
- **Caps:** 6 views a day, 90 s apart; an offer declined isn't shown again for 10 minutes; at most one offer a card;
  none in a visit's first 2 cards; no fill (or an error) grants the reward anyway, twice a day at most.
- **The stand-in ad** (v2 is a test, on the owner's word): no ad network and no video. Where an ad would play, a
  full-screen `.ks-ad` says so ("An ad would play here", the reward it pays for, the placement), with a 5 s countdown
  on time.js's clock (KS_FAST runs it fast) and a close button that gives the reward up, so the owner sees exactly where
  and how often ads would come. Your numbers counts them: stand-in ads a day, by placement. Tests set `window.KS_AD` to `reward`, `dismiss`, `nofill` or
  `error`. Offer buttons carry no `data-test-go`, so the v1 test player never presses one.

### The offers (`.ks-offer`: a button that names its reward; never while a `.ks-ask` is on screen)

| Offer | Where | Reward | Free path | Cap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Revive** | a story lost (a scene's or chat's lose end, a caller hanging up, a chase catching you: before its caught lines play) | the line lost on, again, with attempt 2's help (the frame), never the answer, and the story goes on from there; a chase resumes 10 m clear; no heart lost | ↻ Start over (a heart); 1 free revive a day | 3 a day |
| **A heart** | the hearts card | +1 heart | wait for the next; practise 3 review lines | 3 a day |
| **Episode now** | a series teaser whose next episode is out tomorrow | tomorrow's episode today | wait | 1 a day |
| **Replay a choice** | the end card of a story with a real fork (two different winning endings) | the other branch from the fork and its ending | none | the 6 |
| **Streak** | the streak card (a freeze, or a broken streak) | +1 freeze; or the streak mended within 48 h | milestone freezes | 1 a week; 1 a month |

- **story.js `from(steps, beatId)`:** the steps from a beat on, as a run would reach them (along the path the story
  took to it), so a revive asks that beat again and a replay takes the other branch. A revive never makes the line
  harder, and the rows it brings about carry `ad: true` (rank.js ignores them; the learner model hears the retry as the
  second attempt it is).
- **The wall:** rank.js imports neither econ.js nor ads.js; `fresh()` and `released()` are all the ranker is told.
  rank-check's determinism test runs with ks.econ, ks.ads, `ad` rows, every heart count and every KS_AD, and the picks
  are the same.
- **Honest difficulty:** hearts make a loss cost something, so the ranker must never deal more of them: if more than
  20% of the last 20 stories were lost, the next card starts a rung easier (assist.js), ads on or off; the sim shows
  the loss rate is the same with ads on and off, and with hearts and without.
