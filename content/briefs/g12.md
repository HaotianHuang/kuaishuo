## 打错了 ep 3 信

```
question  What does the letter say?
verbs     read aloud (那天晚上，我来了。 / 每年今天，打这个电话。) · react (就差十分钟) · comfort (不是你的错) · answer
turn      they came at 8:40; 程远 left at 8:30; the letter says call this number every year, and they reached you
fork      去找吧 (find) / 也许……你已经找到了 (here, then a long hold)   sets red-star.ep3
callback  meet → 我在马路对面……不敢过去; phone → 我在店里。信在我手上。
```
Reading beats: a narration line shows the letter's text; the player reads it aloud. L1 reads the core phrase, L3 the
whole line.

```
question  What do you say to a father who left?

turn      林老师, aside, flat: 他儿子昨天来过。订的是同一场。
```

The player plays a role (老黄's son): the lines are neutral, and the player is never gendered as themselves.
