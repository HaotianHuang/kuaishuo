# Brief p4b-1: first words (L0 variants of the ten easiest v1 stories)

ids: hotpot-spicy airport-esim locked-out farm-goose village-tiger office-boss flat-flood courier-crabs mum-calling hotel-wakeup

**For the owner to approve before anything is drafted.** v1 has no level 0 at all: a learner starting from zero
meets 服务员！太辣了！ as their first line. This batch lets the same ten stories play at level 0 (pre-A1): the
same scene, the same voices, only the player's line shrinks to one chunk. Nothing about the stories at their own level
changes, and nothing ships until you've played it (`review/gate/p4b-1.md`).

## How an L0 line is written (CONTENT.md, `levels`)

- **One chunk, 4 characters or fewer** where it can be (a number or a set chunk may run to 5 or 6: 是我！三号出口！).
  Said by a native in a hurry, not a textbook stub.
- **The same moment.** The next line someone says must still follow; where the base line carries something the
  reply needs (the flat number, where the water comes from), the L0 line keeps it.
- **One need group.** Widened, never narrowed, so that every answer the beat accepts today still passes at L0
  (judge-check fails the batch otherwise: someone who says more is never failed).
- **No clock** (the level's default: the model line plays first), a `gloss` for each new line, `"status": "draft"`.
- **Branches stay, with an L0 line**, where they're the joke (the cold beer, the passport): `"alts": [{"of": 0, …}]`.
  docs/v2/content.md suggested hiding branches at L0; I'd keep them, because a choice is half the fun and the lines
  are as easy as the main one. *Your call: keep (my recommendation) or hide.*
- Lines already that short stay as they are, with no override (走开！, 有人吗？, 救命啊！, 不要香菜！ …).

## Size and checks

- 42 L0 lines and 10 branch lines: about 100 new model-voice clips (normal and slow), about 1.3 MB of audio in the
  full catalogue; the pocket edition chooses later what it carries.
- `node tools/factory.mjs p4b-1`: validate, a build into `review/gate/p4b-1/`, judge-check over every override, both
  recognisers on every new clip (a doubted model answer fails the batch).
- Then `review/gate/p4b-1.md`: each story at L0 to play, and what to listen for.
