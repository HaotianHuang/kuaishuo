# Brief p4b-4: one level up and one down, for the ten stories of p4b-1

ids: hotpot-spicy airport-esim locked-out farm-goose village-tiger office-boss flat-flood courier-crabs mum-calling hotel-wakeup

**For the owner to approve before anything is drafted; drafted only after p4b-1 is through its gate** (these are
the same files). The plan gives every story a variant one level up and one down (docs/v2/README.md §9), so a
learner at any level has about 38 story-levels to play. p4b-1 gave these ten their level 0; this batch adds the rest:

| story | level | p4b-1 added | this batch adds |
|---|---|---|---|
| hotpot-spicy, farm-goose, courier-crabs | 1 | 0 | 2 |
| airport-esim, locked-out, village-tiger, office-boss, flat-flood, mum-calling, hotel-wakeup | 2 | 0 | 1 and 3 |

p4b-5 does the same for the other ten v1 stories.

## Down a level

- The same moment in fewer words: drop a clause, a softener or a second need group. Every answer the beat accepts
  today still passes (judge-check holds the batch to it).
- L1 is a set phrase of 3 to 6 characters (不要香菜, 再说一遍, 稍等一下).
- Example, airport-esim/borrow at L1: 能借一下手机吗？ (need: 借, 用, 打电话 · 手机, 电话).

## Up a level

- The same moment, more fully, with the level's own move: L2 one pattern (能…吗, 把…, 被…); L3 deflect, soften or
  refuse (到时候再说吧, 您别操心了, 不好意思，…); L4 persuade or a 俗语; L5 register (a toast, a formal refusal).
- One more need group, at most, for what the longer line adds; the reply that follows must still fit.
- The harder line must still pass the easier levels' needs (it says at least as much).
- Examples:
  - airport-esim/borrow at L3: 大叔，不好意思，能借您手机打个电话吗？我的没信号了。 (need + 信号, 网)
  - mum-calling/newyear at L3: 这个还真不好说，到时候看情况吧。 (a softer, vaguer no)
  - hotel-wakeup/nothanks at L3: 不用了，谢谢您，我想再睡一会儿。 (the polite no with a reason)
  - hotpot-spicy/ordered at L2: 我点的是微辣，你们上错了吧？ (the 是…的 pattern, a gentle accusation)
  - village-tiger/bike at L3 (a chase stays short): 大哥，车借我用一下，马上还！

## Branches

Branches keep their place; where a branch's line would be out of level, the override changes its line too
(`"alts": [{"of": 0, …}]`). No branch is added or removed.

## Size and checks

About 85 overrides and 170 clips; `node tools/factory.mjs p4b-4`; `review/levels.md` shows each new line's measured
level next to its key, and a line two levels off is rewritten before the gate.
