## Beats

Setting: the lift, 1:03 am, the next night. Stage 🛗, blue-black. Others' lines 12 characters or fewer.

1. Narration: the stairwell's locked, so it's the lift. 电梯: 请说楼层。
2. **Command** (L0 十楼。 · L1 去十楼。 · L2 我要去十楼物业。): the tone beat. A `not` for 四楼 (sì against shí). Its
   `fail`: the doors open on fog and the lift says 四楼，到了。, then the beat comes back. Its `miss` is the lose: you step
   out into the fog (a jump, "run that back").
3. The lift stops at 三楼. 孙奶奶 gets in. Her first line reads `dont-answer.door`: leave → 汤圆好吃吗？; ask →
   你问她是谁了？; else → 这么晚还出去啊？ Then 我也住这栋楼。
4. **Ask** (几楼？ · 您住几楼？ · 奶奶，您住几楼啊？). 孙奶奶: 四楼。 `hold`. There's no 四 button. The lights flicker
   (`buzz`); the doors open on dark between floors, and she gets out.
5. 十楼. 物业, in person: 这么晚？怎么了？
6. **Ask** (你打的？ · 你打电话了吗？ · 昨晚你给我打电话了？). 物业: 我没给你打电话啊。 The turn.
7. **Ask** (谁住过？ · 谁住过八零四？ · 八零四以前住的是谁？): an echo of episode 1's 我住过八零四. 物业: 没人住。空了二十年。
8. 物业: 你在电梯里看见谁了？ **The fork:**
   - 一个奶奶。 (tell): he goes quiet and takes out an old photo. 是她吗？ Sets `"told"`.

     safe). On `told`, the photo's back says 孙.
