## 我听得懂 ep 1 百分之十 (§7B)

```
question  Will they get away with ten percent?
stakes    a contract agreed by email, and a room that thinks you understand nothing
verbs     greet (不辛苦) · catch it (百分之十吗？, its need from the aside) · hold or trade
turn      they exchange a look: you heard
fork      说好的价格，不能变 (held) / 百分之五，要早发货 (traded)   sets i-understand.price
secret    讲快点我也听得懂 (heard): she laughs, 好！我就喜欢听得懂的。
out       小周's note: 陈总不是老板。   next: "The real owner wants to see you, at the tea farm, alone."
```

The asides are `pace: fast` on a cast `speed` of 1.05 (1.2, the most ElevenLabs takes). 陈总's aside doesn't gender
the player: 中文还行嘛。价格加百分之十，讲快点，听不出来的。 Her reason is a half line only: 签字的人，要看得懂。上次那个……算了。

## 彩排 ep 1 押金

```
question  Can you get the deposit back from someone who always changes the subject?
stakes    five thousand yuan; tea, an apple, a single relative's WeChat every time you start
verbs     ask (押金什么时候退？) · insist (合同上写着呢) · refuse (不用了，谢谢)
turn      the actor playing 冯阿姨 is 冯阿姨, rehearsing how not to pay you; 林老师: 两边都是客户。
fork      我们都别演了 (straight) / 再来一遍 (again)   sets rehearsal.deposit
out       next: "林老师 has built your parents' living room. Your mother is played by a man called 老黄."
```

The nephew of the sketch is 亲戚, a relative: a matchmaking offer that doesn't assume who you date.
