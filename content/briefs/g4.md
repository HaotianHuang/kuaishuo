## 别回答: the season's answer

Twenty years ago 孙奶奶 lived in 804 with 小安, the child she raised, who moved abroad and stopped calling. She has
called 小安's name at night ever since; every tenant thought she was calling them, and wrote 别回答. In episode 1 she
asked 你叫什么名字？ to find out if you were 小安. The notes are in each tenant's own hand: said once, in episode 5's
turn. 小安 is never gendered.

### Ep 5 规则 The Rules

```
question  Which rule is false?
stakes    物业: 中元那天晚上，她叫得最响。
verbs     ask (以前住这儿的人呢？) · read aloud (别回答 or 别跑; reading 别说四 says 四, and the lift dings) · name it (第一条是假的) · decide
turn      behind the wardrobe, 阿梅's list from ten years ago: rule one crossed out, 回答我 beside it; 物业: everyone's notes were in their own hand
fork      我自己等她 (alone) / 你陪我等吧 (together)   sets dont-answer.rules
out       next: "Midnight on the fifteenth. Someone outside 804 is saying a name."
callback  dont-answer.stairs: together → 小郑 helps search; hid → 小郑：我还藏着呢……
```

Naming the rule is an ordinary beat with a right answer. Its `miss`: you say rule two aloud, 四, and the lift stops at
four again.

## 假鬼社 The Fake Ghost Club, ep 1 火锅店的鬼 (genres.md §7C)

A new show, content/series/fake-ghosts.json, `genre: mystery`: 阿乐, 小雨, 果果, 包子 (the cat), and for this case 蒋叔,
小潘 and 小芳. No names from world.json.

```
question  Who is the ghost in the cold room?
stakes    2 am, after closing, and 蒋叔 is shaking
verbs     ask (什么时候开始的？) · ask (两点你在哪儿？) · set a trap (打小潘的电话！) · accuse (是小潘！) · decide
turn      the ringtone, from inside the cold room
fork      出来吧，我们不说出去 (gentle) / 蒋叔，鬼在冷库里 (told)   sets fake-ghosts.hotpot
out       a note under the club's door: 下一个鬼是真的。
```

Fair play: the ringtone is heard twice (from the cold room, then 小潘's phone as he leaves) before the trap. The
accusation is an ordinary beat; its comic miss points at 包子. 小潘's reason, his grandmother's chilli sauce, is the
same in both branches. 果果 is in the show but not in this episode's cast (no lines yet).
`fake-ghosts.hotpot` is read in this episode's ending until episode 2 reads it.
