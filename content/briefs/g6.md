## 打错了 ep 1 红星饭店

```
question  Why does someone book a table at a restaurant that closed ten years ago?
stakes    they're about to go and wait outside what's now a phone repair stall
verbs     answer (哪位？) · correct (你打错了) · explain (早就关门了) · comfort (一点也不奇怪) · decide
turn      every year, on this day: ten years ago they waited there, and that person never came
fork      那……祝你好运 (luck) / 要不要……换个地方？ (offer)   sets red-star.ep1
out       next: "A week later, your phone rings at 7:30. The same number."
```

程远 is never gendered in the lines; nor is the one who never came. Charming (哈，你一定觉得我很奇怪。 你这是在约我吗？),
never pitiful. The call's own nudges and hang-up: 你那边……好安静。 and, hung up on, next year the phone rings again.
