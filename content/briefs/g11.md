## 远大前程 ep 1 滩涂

```
question  What will you do for a stranger who frightens you?
stakes    food and 钳子 by dawn; 我不是一个人。外面还有人。
verbs     plead (别伤害我！) · bargain (我去拿，你等着) · lie (是我吃的，我太饿了) · decide
turn      at dawn: 你叫什么名字？……我会记住你的。
fork      钳子也给你 (pliers) / 只有吃的 (food)   sets expectations.ep1
```
Frightening, ends safe; no police, only the search boats' lights. 舅妈 strict, 舅舅 kind (孩子长身体呢).

## 穿越 ep 1 长安

```
question  How do you survive your first evening in 长安?
```

L0: the player's lines 1 to 4 characters (饼多少钱 and 我能住吗 are 4); everyone else 8 or fewer, at speed 0.85.
