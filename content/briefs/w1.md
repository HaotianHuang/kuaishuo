## back-home-date 相亲 / The Blind-Date Ambush (scene; Back Home 4; dating, family)

- **Level** 3 (variants 2 and 4), listen 3. **Setting:** a tea house in town, Sunday, 3 pm. 二姨 said "just tea".
- **Stakes:** across the table sits 刘阿姨, the mother of someone 二姨 has picked for you (ep 3's cliffhanger: she sent
  your photo to someone's mother). The child isn't here: 刘阿姨 interviews you for them, as at a park 相亲 corner. Her
  child is never named or gendered (我家孩子), so the player is never gendered either.
- **Callback** (the opening, `when` on `back-home.job`): 二姨 introduces you as 我们家的技术员 (`"fixed"`: you fixed
  奶奶's phone) or as the one who designed 大姑's shop sign (`"sign"`); with neither (a player who skipped ep 3), as 在上海
  工作的.
- **Your lines** (L3: deflect, soften, refuse kindly): a polite greeting (阿姨好，久仰久仰) → her questions, one after
  another: your job (said the way ep 3 taught you), your flat (租的，在上海), can you cook (会一点，饺子包得不好) → 你看我家
  孩子怎么样？ the fork.
- **Fork:**
  - 先加个微信，慢慢聊吧。 (keep it open) → 刘阿姨 beams and shows you 47 photos of her child. Sets
    `back-home.blind-date: "wechat"`; 二姨 +1. **Ending A.**
  - 阿姨，我现在不想找对象，但是谢谢您。 (honest, kind) → 刘阿姨 laughs: her child said exactly the same this morning.
    She writes down her dumpling recipe for you instead. Sets `"no"`; 二姨 −1 (she's thinking). **Ending B.**
  - **Secret** (not offered; heard if said): 您家孩子是不是也不想来？ → 刘阿姨 whispers 被你说中了: the two of you agree to
    tell 二姨 it went beautifully. Sets `"pact"`. **Secret ending.**
- **next:** 奶奶 has fallen in the kitchen. She says it's nothing; 表哥 is already driving her to the hospital.

## landlords-mahjong 三缺一 / One Player Short (scene; The Landlords 1; home, celebrations)

- **Level** 2 (variants 1 and 3), listen 2. **Setting:** 王叔 and 王阿姨's flat, two floors down, Friday, 7 pm. Since
  rent-rise, 王阿姨 has taken up square dancing, so 王叔's mahjong table is one short.
- **Stakes:** 王叔 knocks: 三缺一！ His two friends are already shuffling. The stakes are sunflower seeds and the washing
  up, nothing else (no money anywhere in it).
- **Callback** (`when` on `rent-rise.renew`): 王叔 tells his friends 这就是续了一年的那个好租客！ (the good tenant who renewed
  for a year); without it, 这是楼上的租客 (the tenant upstairs).
- **Your lines:** 我不太会打。 (I'm not very good) → 碰！ (call a tile: the one-word line the table teaches you) → asking
  what a tile is (这个叫什么？) → 王阿姨 walks in from dancing, sees you about to win → the fork.
- **Fork:**
  - 王叔，您打这张吧。 (let him win, nudging him to the winning tile) → 王叔 wins, crows, and tells the whole building he
    taught you. Sets `landlords.mahjong: "let-win"`; 王叔 +1. **Ending A.**
  - 胡了！ (win it) → silence, then 王阿姨 cheers louder than anyone: someone finally beat him. You wash up anyway. Sets
    `"won"`; 王阿姨 +1. **Ending B.**
- **next:** 王叔 has a rematch planned, and 王阿姨 wants a word about the dance team.

## slow-burn-hotpot 第一次见面 / The First Date (scene; The Slow Burn 1; dating, food)

- **Level** 2 (variants 1 and 3), listen 2. **Setting:** the hotpot place from first-date, Saturday, 7 pm. 小鹿 is
  outside with 大白 the goose, pink bow and all.
- **Callback** (`when` on `first-date.tease`): 小鹿's first words: 你上次说你都看到了……我到现在还社死！ (still mortified you saw
  the unsent message); without it, 终于见面了！
- **Your lines:** say hi in person (你好，我是……) → ordering the pot together (要鸳鸯锅吧？) → 大白 bites the waiter: smooth
  it over (不好意思，它不是故意的) → 小鹿: 我们这……算约会吗？ the fork.
- **Fork:** (who you are with someone; both warm, and the thread follows either)
  - 算！下次我请你。 → 小鹿 goes pink to match the bow. Sets `slow-burn.path: "date"`; 小鹿 +1. **Ending A.**
  - 先做朋友吧，慢慢来。 → 小鹿 smiles, relieved: friends first, then. 大白 approves. Sets `"friends"`; 小鹿 +1. **Ending
    B.**
- **next:** 小鹿's mum has seen a photo of the two of you, and the goose.

## who-pays 谁买单 / Who's Paying? (scene; You Decide; food, family)

- **Level** 3 (variant 2), listen 3; heritage. **Setting:** a restaurant, the end of dinner with 小林's parents, who
  are visiting Shanghai. The bill arrives; 林阿姨 and you both reach for it.
- **The dilemma** (30 seconds): 林阿姨 has the bill; you have your phone out; 林叔叔 is pretending to look at the menu.
  The tug of war is the most ordinary fight in China.
- **The verdict** (`"verdict": true`, said to 林阿姨):
  - 阿姨，这次让我来！ (insist) → the tug of war, three rounds, until 林叔叔 takes the bill and pays it himself. **Ending A.**
  - 那下次我请您！ (accept, and promise) → 林阿姨 holds you to it, in front of witnesses. Sets `who-pays.verdict:
    "promised"` (a message from 小林 can hold you to it in a later batch). **Ending B.**
  - 我已经付过了。 (you paid at the counter on the way back from the toilet) → 林阿姨 is outraged, then delighted:
    你这孩子！ **Ending C.**
- Every verdict is a fine thing to say. Later (P6, with a server), the end card can show what other players said.

## Rules the batch follows

- CONTENT.md's style rules, and the world's: every option is a fine thing to say; low feelings are never cruel;
  characters miss you warmly and never scold an absence; nothing expires.
- Branch and bottleneck: a fork changes the next scene's opening lines, the end card's teaser and a message; no whole
  other episode.
- Every flag set is read in this batch (validate holds it to that); every `when` has its `else`, so a player who
  skipped the story it remembers still gets a whole scene.
- `content/world.json`'s cast, with their voices; 林阿姨, 林叔叔 and 刘阿姨 are new and stay strangers (not in the bible)
  unless a later batch brings them back.
