# Brief p4b-3: 回老家 Back Home, episodes 1 to 3

ids: back-home-pickup back-home-cute back-home-job

**For the owner to approve before anything is drafted.** The first heritage series. You fly back to your parents'
hometown for 奶奶's eightieth birthday, and the family descends. Eight episodes are planned (docs/v2/README.md §9),
from level 2 to level 5; this batch is the first three. They're written listen-first: the relatives talk fast and a
lot (listen 3, no slowing down), and your lines are short replies that carry the culture (嗯嗯, 到了到了, 不用不用,
没有啦). Each episode ends on a cliffhanger (`series.next`) and has one real fork with its own ending, because the
plan's "replay the other choice" offer needs a different ending to show.

The build gives each of them one voice in every episode. The town is unnamed and ordinary: a high-speed rail station,
a new mall, an old courtyard, a night market. The player is never gendered (对象, 孩子, and 奶奶's 宝贝).

## Episode 1: back-home-pickup 表哥来接你 / The Pickup (call; family, travel)

- **Level** 2 (variants 1 and 3), listen 3. **Setting:** the town's small airport, 10:40 pm, after 14 hours from
  Toronto. Your phone rings before the plane has stopped.
- **Stakes:** 表哥 is outside, at the wrong exit, talking non-stop: is the plane down, which exit, are you hungry, did
  you bring the gifts, 奶奶's been waiting since six, have you got a 对象 yet…
- **Your lines:** pick up (喂，表哥！我到了！) → where you are (我在二号出口。) → he wants to come in and carry your
  bags: 不用不用，我自己拿得动。 → the 对象 question: deflect (还没有，别问啦！) → the fork.
- **Fork:** 先回家还是先吃夜宵？
  - 先回家吧，奶奶等着呢。 → 奶奶 at the gate in her coat, a pot of chicken soup kept warm since eight. **Ending A.**
  - 先吃夜宵！ → 表哥 drives to a crayfish stall where the whole family is already sitting: an ambush with chopsticks.
    **Ending B.**
- **next:** 奶奶 has invited the whole street to lunch tomorrow. They all want to hear your Chinese.

## Episode 2: back-home-cute 你中文好可爱 / Your Chinese Is So Cute (scene; family, celebrations)

- **Level** 2 (variants 1 and 3), listen 3 (several relatives at once). **Setting:** 奶奶's courtyard, Saturday lunch,
  twelve relatives and three neighbours, a table of twenty dishes.
- **Stakes:** everyone wants to hear you speak, all at once; 大姑's camera is filming for the group chat.
- **Your lines:** greet the table (奶奶好！大家好！) → 你中文好可爱！: the comeback (我的中文是奶奶教的！ 奶奶 beams;
  the joke lands on nobody) → 会用筷子吗？ (我从小就用！) → 二姨: 是不是胖了？ (奶奶做的菜太好吃了！) → the fork.
- **Fork:** 给大家表演一个！ (perform something for everyone)
  - 我唱首歌吧！ → you sing 《月亮代表我的心》 and the whole table joins in, off key and loud. **Ending A.**
  - 我讲个笑话！ → a one-line joke in Chinese; 奶奶 laughs until she cries, and makes you tell it again. **Ending B.**
- **next:** In the kitchen, 大姑 is cornering your mum: she wants to know exactly what you do for a living.
- The style rule this episode exists for: "你中文好可爱" gets a comeback the player lands. Nobody laughs at the player's
  Chinese; the relatives are proud and a bit nosy.

## Episode 3: back-home-job 你到底做什么的？/ So What Do You Actually Do? (chat; family, work)

- **Level** 2 (variants 1 and 3), listen 2 (text, two voice notes). **Setting:** the next morning, the family WeChat
  group 相亲相爱一家人 (23 members, four time zones) and a private chat with 大姑.
- **Stakes:** 大姑 asks what your job is. Whatever you answer, she will tell the group.
- **Your lines:** 我是做设计的。 → she hears 修手机 (she runs a phone shop): 不是修手机，是设计软件！ → 就是手机上用的那些。
  → the group fills with photos of relatives' cracked phones → the fork.
- **Fork:**
  - 我试试吧。 → you fix 奶奶's phone by turning it off and on again; the group crowns you 家里的技术员. **Ending A.**
  - 我真的不会修！ → 大姑: 那你给我的店设计个牌子！ You sketch her shop sign on a napkin; it's up by lunchtime.
    **Ending B.**
- **next:** 二姨 has already sent your photo to someone's mother.

## Rules every episode follows

- CONTENT.md's style rules; relatives pry because they care, and each ending is warm.
- Heritage first: relatives' lines up to about 20 characters at natural speed, a `"pace": "fast"` line or two; your
  model answers 5 to 10 characters at L2, set phrases at L1 (嗯嗯，到了！ 不用不用！), and at L3 the softer, deflecting
  version (到时候再说吧, 您别操心了).
- The fork is an `alts` branch whose `then` ends in its own `end` (as hotel-wakeup's breakfast does); the main line
  is the other ending.
- `series: {"id": "back-home", "ep": n, "next": "…"}`, `"audience": "heritage"`, `topics` as above.
