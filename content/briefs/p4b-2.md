# Brief p4b-2: eight new level-0 stories

ids: bubble-tea night-market breakfast-qr toilet-dash tour-group square-dance driver-chat wrong-number

**For the owner to approve before anything is drafted.** Eight stories written for level 0 from the start: the
player's lines are one chunk each (多少钱？, 少糖, 不是我！), everyone else talks in short, slow lines (8 characters or
fewer, `"speed": 0.85`, English on), and each story still has stakes by second five and a twist at the end. Each also
gets a level-1 variant of every line (one level up), so it grows with the learner. All `"status": "draft"`,
`"audience": "new"`, voiced by the existing cast pools (no new voices).

The mix follows docs/v2/content.md's L0 row: three scenes, three chases, one chat, one call. Settings spread from a
Toronto mall to Tianjin, Chongqing, Xi'an, Shanghai and London.

## The stories

### 1. bubble-tea 一杯奶茶 / One Bubble Tea, Please (scene; food)
- **Setting:** Pacific Mall, Markham (Toronto), Saturday 3 pm. A queue of twenty behind you.
- **Cast:** 阿姨 (f-mid), who runs the stall and only speaks Mandarin, fast and kind; a kid in the queue (kid).
- **Stakes:** the queue is growing and 阿姨 fires questions: 要什么？ 几分糖？ 冰呢？ 加珍珠吗？
- **Your lines:** order (一杯奶茶！), sugar (少糖！), ice (去冰！), pearls (要！ or 不要！, a branch: no pearls gets a
  look of pure pity), thanks (谢谢阿姨！).
- **Twist:** she writes 少糖去冰 on the cup as your name, and the kid behind you orders 跟这个一样！ An egg tart on the
  house: 第一次来吧？送你的！ (warm).
- **L1:** 我要一杯奶茶。 少糖，去冰。 珍珠多一点！ 谢谢阿姨，下次再来！

### 2. night-market 太贵了！/ That's Too Much! (scene; shopping)
- **Setting:** a night market by the river in Chengdu, 9 pm.
- **Cast:** 老板 (m-mid), selling panda hats, a born showman.
- **Stakes:** your friend's birthday is tomorrow and the hat is perfect; he says 一百.
- **Your lines:** 多少钱？ → 太贵了！ → 便宜点！ → 五十！ → 好，要了！
- **Twist:** you "win" at 五十 and walk off proud, past the next stall: same hat, 三十. Your 老板 waves: 下次再来！
  and throws in a panda keyring anyway (送你的！). Nobody's the butt: he's charming, you're happy.
- **L1:** 这个多少钱？ 太贵了吧！ 便宜一点吧？ 五十行不行？ 好，就要这个！

### 3. breakfast-qr 扫不了 / It Won't Scan (scene; food, shopping)
- **Setting:** a 煎饼果子 stall in Tianjin, 7:40 am. You're late for work.
- **Cast:** 大爷 (m-old) at the griddle; 阿姨 (f-mid) behind you in the queue.
- **Stakes:** he's already cracking the egg; your phone's payment code won't scan.
- **Your lines:** 一个煎饼！ → 加个蛋！ → 不要辣！ → 扫不了！ (the code fails twice) → 谢谢阿姨！
- **Twist:** 阿姨 scans hers: 我来！ and as you thank her: 明天你请我！ Tomorrow's breakfast is booked (warm).
- **L1:** 来一个煎饼。 加一个蛋。 不要辣的。 我的码扫不了！ 谢谢阿姨，明天我请您！

### 4. toilet-dash 厕所在哪儿？/ Where's the Toilet? (chase; food, travel)
- **Setting:** a Chongqing shopping mall, 1 pm, twenty minutes after a hotpot lunch.
- **Chaser:** 🌶️, the hotpot, gaining.
- **Encounters:** a cleaner (厕所在哪儿？), a crowd at the escalator (借过！), a guard pointing the wrong way (不是那边！),
  a closed door (有人吗？, again: 厕所呢？ to a second cleaner), the queue (快点！).
- **Finale:** a voice inside says 有人！; the door next to it was free all along. The shop outside sells
  火锅味冰淇淋 (hotpot-flavoured ice cream). Win.
- **L1:** 请问厕所在哪儿？ 借过一下！ 不对，不是那边！ 里面有人吗？ 麻烦快一点！

### 5. tour-group 我不是你们团的！/ Not in Your Group! (chase; travel)
- **Setting:** outside the Terracotta Army museum, Xi'an, noon.
- **Chaser:** 🚩 a tour guide (f-mid) with a megaphone, and her whole group, because you're wearing their red cap.
- **Encounters:** 不是！ → 我不是！ → 不用了！ → 我自己走！ → again: 我不是！ → 再见！
- **Finale:** she corners you at the gate with the group's free dumpling lunch: 一起吃吧！ You: 好啊！ The group adopts
  you; you're in their photo. Warm.
- **L1:** 不是不是！ 我不是你们团的！ 谢谢，不用了！ 我自己走就行！ 再见啦！

### 6. square-dance 一起跳！/ Dance With Us! (chase; celebrations)
- **Setting:** the square of a Shanghai housing compound, 7 pm.
- **Chaser:** 💃 the 广场舞 aunties (f-mid, f-old) and their speaker, who need one more dancer.
- **Encounters:** 我不会！ → 不跳！ → 我很忙！ → 明天吧！ → again: 我不会！
- **Finale:** you trip into the front row; they cheer; you: 好吧！ Twist: you're a natural, and by the end you're the
  one they follow. Warm (they're delighted, not mocking).
- **L1:** 我真的不会跳！ 我不跳了！ 我现在很忙！ 明天吧，明天一定！ 好吧，就一首！

### 7. driver-chat 我在门口 / I'm by the Door (chat; travel)
- **Setting:** Chongqing Jiangbei airport, arrivals, 11 pm; the ride-hailing app's chat with your driver.
- **Cast:** 司机 (m-mid), typing in short bursts, with one voice note.
- **Your replies** (tiles at L0, typed at L1): 我在门口！ → 三号门！ → 红衣服！ → he sends a photo of a stranger in red:
  是你吗？ → 不是我！ → 谢谢师傅！
- **Twist:** the last photo is him, waving a sign with your name in characters (written very large).
- **L1:** 我在出口这儿。 三号门，出来就是。 我穿红衣服。 不是我，那不是我！ 看到了，谢谢师傅！

### 8. wrong-number 打错了 / Wrong Number (call; family)
- **Setting:** London, Sunday 7 pm. A number from China.
- **Cast:** 奶奶 (f-old) in Harbin, calling her grandchild 小宝, slowly and happily.
- **Stakes:** she's already telling you about her day and asking when you're coming home.
- **Your lines:** 喂？ → 我不是小宝！ → 打错了！ → (she asks if you've eaten anyway) 吃了！ → 奶奶，再见！
- **Twist:** on her end a voice shouts 奶奶！我在这儿！: 小宝 was in the next room all along. She laughs and tells you to
  eat more too: 那你也多吃点啊！ Warm.
- **L1:** 喂，您好？ 不好意思，我不是小宝。 您打错了。 吃过了，谢谢奶奶！ 奶奶，您保重，再见！

## Rules every story follows

- The style rules in CONTENT.md: nobody's accent or identity is the joke; China ordinary and modern; warm endings.
- L0 lines: one chunk, 4 characters or fewer where it can be; one need group; no clock (the model line plays first).
- The L1 variant of each beat: 3 to 6 characters, a set phrase; every L1 answer passes the L0 beat too.
- Every NPC line 8 characters or fewer, at most 10 lines before the end; cast `"speed": 0.85`.
- `hook` scored by a judge agent after drafting.

## Size and checks

About 45 clips a story plus 10 for its L1 lines: roughly 450 clips, 8 minutes of voicing, about 5 MB of audio in the
full catalogue. `node tools/factory.mjs p4b-2`, then `review/gate/p4b-2.md` for you to play.
