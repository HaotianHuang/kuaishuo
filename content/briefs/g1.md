## The show

`content/series/dont-answer.json`: a show (genres.md §2), `genre: horror`, `optin: true`, its own cast and home, outside
world.json. Your first night in a short-let flat, 804, in an old 小区 on a hillside in 重庆, in the seventh lunar month.
The season question: who is calling your name, and how do they know it? The rules arrive one an episode; one is false.

- 物业 `wuye`: the property office, heard on the phone (`phone: true`), m-mid.
- 门外的人 `voice`: a voice outside the door who knows your flat number, f-old. Who she is is the season's answer (ep 6),
  so ep 1 gives nothing away but 我住过八零四.

## Beats

Setting: flat 804, 11:58 pm. Stage 🚪, a blue-black gradient. Everyone else at L0's pace (`speed` 0.85), lines of 8
characters or fewer.

1. Narration: 八零四，第一晚。 The phone rings. 物业: 喂，八零四吗？
2. **Confirm** (L0 对。 · L1 对，是八零四。 · L2 对，我住八零四。) Cue: "Yes, it's 804."
3. 物业: 今晚停水。 门锁好了吗？
4. **Reassure** (锁好了。 · 门锁好了。 · 门已经锁好了。) Cue: "Door's locked." A `not` for 没锁.
5. 物业: 如果有人叫你…… The line crackles (`buzz`) and drops (`hold` 2).
6. `knock`. 门外的人: 外卖！
7. **Refuse** (我没点。 · 我没点外卖。 · 你送错了，我没点外卖。) Cue: "You didn't order anything." Its `miss` is the
   episode's lose: the locked door opens anyway, a jump and a "run that back".
8. 门外的人: 八零四，没错啊。 Then, softer: 你叫什么名字？ (`hold` 1)
9. **The fork** (L0 放门口吧。 / 你是谁？ · L1 放门口就行。 / 你到底是谁？ · L2 东西放门口就行。 / 你到底是什么人？). A
   `not` for 我叫……: rule one is already in the air.
   - Leave it: footsteps, then nothing. On the mat, a bowl of 汤圆, still warm, and a note: 第一条：别回答。
     Sets `dont-answer.door: "leave"`.
   - Who are you: 我？我住过八零四。 The lights come back on. The phone rings: 物业, cheerful: 别回答。晚安！
     Sets `"ask"`.
   - Its `miss` (no answer twice): she sighs, and the 汤圆 are left at the door all the same.
   - Left out until the engine has `hush`: the secret of answering with silence.
- **next:** "The note is in your handwriting."

## Rules it keeps

- Horror's (genres.md §6): ends safe (warm 汤圆, the lights, a cheerful goodnight), no gore, no religion or rites; sound
  does the work with today's sounds only (ring, buzz, knock, door, heartbeat) and `hold`.
- The flag `dont-answer.door` is read in episode 2 (the callback). Until episode 2 exists, the validator's "set but
  never read" is expected.
